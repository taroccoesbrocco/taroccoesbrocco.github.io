Curry-Howard and the lambda-Calculus

An introductory mini-course inside the course Advanced Topics in ACP (Odense, Denmark, 21-29 October 2026), by Giulio Guerrieri.

  • Lecture 1: Natural deduction for minimal logic and cut-elimination. Slides. Solutions to exercises.

  • Lecture 2: The simply typed lambda-calculus and the Curry-Howard correspondence. Slides. Solutions to exercises.

  • Lecture 3: The untyped lambda-calculus. Slides. Solutions to exercises.

  • Lecture 4: Non-idempotent intersection types for the lambda-calculus. Slides. Solutions to exercises.
